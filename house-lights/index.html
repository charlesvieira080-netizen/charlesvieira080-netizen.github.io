The board does the work

You pick a square, read the clue and open the buzzers. The first press lights up on the TV with the next two tables lined up behind it, so when that answer misses you hit the down arrow and the next table is already up. Double values, a final question with wagers and the winner screen are each one key away, and if you hit the wrong one, Z takes it back.

Tables keep their own names and scores all night, and if House Lights or the browser crashes mid-show, it picks up where it stopped.

Phones as buzzers

Turn phones on and the join card goes up on the TV. Guests scan the code, type a first name, pick their table and get one big button, with no app to download and no account to make. A phone press counts the same as a buzzer press, and whichever came first wins.

Where the Wi-Fi is good, the phones talk to your laptop directly. In a hotel ballroom or a bar where it isn't, Anywhere mode carries the presses over the guests' own data, and the laptop only needs your phone's hotspot.

More than Quiz Time

Speed Trivia has every table answering every question, with faster answers worth more. Survey Showdown puts two teams against the top answers on the board, while Music Bingo, Needle Drop and Time the Drop are built around the songs you're already playing. Dead Stop has tables guessing when a hidden clock passes its target, and Bank or Bust, Over Under, Stop the Light and a quiz bowl round fill out the list.

Each game has its own rules panel, so you can turn penalties off for a gentle room or make a bar night meaner.

In the classroom

The same board works for test review days and quiz bowl practice in grades 7 to 12. Students join from a phone or a Chromebook with a first name and a team, so there are no student accounts, and the questions come straight from your own review sheet.

Where the school network keeps devices apart, Anywhere mode runs the presses over the internet instead, and buzzers need no network at all.

Questions DJs ask

If I'm on a Mac, can I run it?

Not yet, so for now you'd run the show from a Windows laptop with Chrome.

Do my guests need to download anything, like an app?

No, they scan the code on the TV and play right in their phone's browser, with nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

Can I use my own questions, or only yours?

Yes, you can write them in the pack editor or bring them in from a spreadsheet saved as tab-separated text, and pictures and audio clips work too.

If the Wi-Fi drops mid-show, what happens?

Buzzers keep working with no internet at all, and if the phones drop, you turn them off, hand those tables buzzers and carry on with the same scores.

When I install it, will Windows warn me?

The first time, Windows may show a SmartScreen notice because the program is new. Click More info, then Run anyway.

If something breaks before a show, who do I call?

Email us at info@cuttyhunkbranding.com and we answer within 1 business day, same day before a show.
