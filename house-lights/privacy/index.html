House Lights privacy notice

What leaves your laptop, and what does not.

House Lights is software that runs on your own computer. This notice explains what, if anything, leaves that computer.

The show itself sends nothing anywhere. Teams, scores, question packs, sounds, pictures and settings are stored in the show's own Chrome profile on your computer (in the HouseLights folder under your local app data). We never see them. Deleting the House Lights program folder you unzipped does not delete that data folder, so your packs survive a fresh unzip; delete it yourself if you want it gone.

The optional phone feature. If you turn phones on in "Anywhere" mode, your computer connects to a relay we run on Cloudflare, and your guests' phones connect to the same relay. For each show the relay holds: a four-letter room code, the first name each guest typed, the table they chose, the cheers and shout-outs they send, their answers to phone bonus questions, and the table scores. This is kept only to run the show. The relay deletes a room, and everything in it, 12 hours after the host was last connected. Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf under its own terms.

In "This Wi-Fi only" mode nothing leaves your network: your computer is the relay and it keeps rooms in memory only until the show ends.

What we do not do. We do not sell or share guest data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track what you do in the Software, and the Software does not send us usage statistics or crash reports.

Your purchase. Your name, email and payment details are handled by our storefront, Gumroad, under its privacy policy; we receive your name, email and order details so we can deliver the Software and help you. We keep those records for as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually seven years.

Children. The Software is sold to businesses and adults who host events. Guests of any age may join a show from a phone at the host's invitation; the relay collects only a first name and a table, and the host can remove any guest.

Contact and changes. Questions to info@cuttyhunkbranding.com, Ensemble Events, New Bedford, Massachusetts. We will post changes to this notice at ensemble-events.com/house-lights/privacy/ with the date.
