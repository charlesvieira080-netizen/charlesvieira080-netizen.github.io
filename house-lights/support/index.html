House Lights support

Most fixes take a minute and live in the Help tab inside the software. Here they are in one place, with how to reach us when they don't do it.

Getting started

Unzip the House Lights folder anywhere and double-click HouseLights.exe. The first time, press Walk me through setup: it opens the TV window, sets your tables and their names, teaches the buzzers, loads a question pack and asks what kind of room you're playing. Move to TV puts the board on the second screen, one click on the board fills it, and Esc brings the frame back.

If Windows shows a SmartScreen notice the first time, click More info, then Run anyway. If it asks whether House Lights can use the network, allow it on private networks so phones on the same Wi-Fi can reach the show.

Phones

Press Turn phones on in the Phones tab, then Show the join card on the TV. This Wi-Fi only works when the guests and the laptop are on the same network and that network lets devices see each other. Anywhere mode works on any phone with a signal, and the laptop needs internet from the venue Wi-Fi or your hotspot. Hotel and bar Wi-Fi often keeps devices apart, so we use Anywhere with a phone hotspot at our own shows.

Before guests sit down, scan the join card with your own phone on cellular, pick a table and do one test press.

Buzzers

Any buzzer set that types a key per buzzer works: buzzers 1 to 9 type 1 to 9, buzzer 10 types 0, and 11 to 24 type A to N. Press Teach all buzzers in the Teams tab and press each one in order. The file buzzer-check.html in the House Lights folder shows exactly what each buzzer sends.

When something goes wrong

The TV went black or shows an old screen

Check the TV button at the top right of the host window. If it's red, the board window closed, so click it to open a new one, drag it over and click it once, and the board catches up on its own.

A buzzer stopped working

Go to the Teams tab, press Teach this buzzer next to that team, then press the buzzer. If none of them work, unplug the receiver and plug it back in, and in a pinch the number keys on the laptop work as stand-ins.

I pressed the wrong thing

Press Z or the Undo button at the top, and it steps back through the scoring one action at a time.

The sound is on the wrong speaker

The first setting on the Sound tab picks this laptop or the TV window, and the TV window needs one click before the browser lets it play sound.

Phones stopped landing

Turn phones off in the Phones tab and hand those tables buzzers, and the scores and the game stay exactly where they were.

House Lights closed mid-show

Double-click HouseLights.exe again and it'll resume where it stopped, since teams, scores and the game in progress are saved on the laptop as they change. A point you gave in the last split second before it closed might not be there, so read the scores out before the next question, and do the same if the laptop itself lost power or restarted.
