Built for a school

Clean, loud, and fair.

  • Clean is the rule. Every question is read before the day, and nothing personal, romantic or edgy makes the cut.
  • Your school's language. The mascot, the teachers who are good sports, and the traditions your students already know.
  • Every homeroom gets a turn. Contestants rotate fast, and nobody gets dragged up who doesn't want to be.
  • Our sound. A speaker that carries a gym, because a school PA was never built for a game show.
  • Short rounds for younger grades, with quiet ways to play alongside the loud ones.
  • Your staff stay in charge. We work with the teachers and the chaperones, never around them.

Questions people ask

The honest answers.

Is the content safe for school?

Yes. Every question is read before the day, clean is the rule, and the principal can see the rounds ahead of time.

What does the gym need?

A screen or projector we can plug into, power, and a corner to set up in. We bring the sound, the buzzers, the games and the host.

Does it work for younger grades?

Yes, with shorter rounds, easier categories and quiet ways to play alongside the loud ones. The phone games are for families and colleges, not for younger students on their own.

How do we pay?

An invoice with a W-9, so the business office can pay it the way it pays every vendor.
