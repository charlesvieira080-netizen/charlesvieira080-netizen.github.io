Your window

Pick a date and the card fills in: sunset, golden hour, last light, when the ceremony should end for daylight photos, and when to step out for sunset portraits.

The rules I use: with no first look, end the ceremony about two hours before sunset for family photos and portraits in daylight. With a first look, an hour before is enough. Outdoors, end by thirty minutes before sunset or the recessional walks out in the dark. Sunset portraits are a fifteen-minute step-out at thirty minutes before sunset.
