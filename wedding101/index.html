Wedding 101

Let's get to work.

There's a lot to do, and time goes by faster than you think. I've spent twenty years and hundreds of weddings in the middle of the room, and I've always said a wedding is a movie production: it's expensive, everyone needs to know their role, and you only get one take. These are the lessons, in the order the decisions come up, with a tool at every point where you have to make one. All of it is free, and none of it is gated.
