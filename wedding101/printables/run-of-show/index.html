The rules I use

  1. The cocktail hour covers the photos. Ask the photographer for the number.
  2. Fifteen minutes to seat a room; twenty-five for two hundred people or another room.
  3. Toasts before dinner, under fifteen minutes all together.
  4. Plated two courses about an hour, a buffet forty-five; ten minutes more for every fifty guests over a hundred.
  5. Cake about thirty minutes after the last plate.
  6. Parent dances right after the cake, while the room is seated.
  7. Last song five minutes before the music has to end; line everyone up for the send-off ten minutes before that.

The faint numbers are my defaults. Write over them. Whatever is left after the formalities is your dance floor; ninety minutes or more is what a five-hour night usually gives it.

Local clock, EST or EDT as the date falls. Golden hour starts an hour before sunset; with no first look the ceremony should end about two hours before sunset for daylight photos, an hour with a first look; outdoors, end thirty minutes before sunset at the latest. Sunset moves about a minute a day, so check the exact minute for your date. Source: US Naval Observatory, 41.6362 N, 70.9342 W, calendar year 2027; within a few minutes for the South Coast, the South Shore, Cape Cod, and Rhode Island.
