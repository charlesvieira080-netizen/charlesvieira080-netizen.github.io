The programs

Four ways to fill the afternoon.

The headliner

The Quiz Show

Five categories from their decades: the songs, the shows, the hometowns. The host reads every clue twice, hands go up, tables play as teams, and the board is on your screen. No buzzers unless the room wants them.

For assisted living, independent living and senior centers. Memory care plays it in shorter rounds.

How it plays

The Survey Showdown

Two teams, one player at a time, the top answers hidden on the board. The television format the room already knows, with no clock to speak of.

How it plays

The Sing-Along

The words on your screen in big type, a wireless mic that comes to the seat, group songs before any solo, and a pass for anyone who would rather listen. Sinatra to the Beatles.

How it runs

The Music Hour

A DJ set from the years the room lived through, requests welcome, at a volume that leaves conversation possible. Room to dance, and no pressure to.

Built for the room

Seated, read aloud, big type.

  • Seated play. Tables as teams, residents where they already are.
  • Read aloud, twice. Every question and every answer on a microphone.
  • Big type, high contrast. The board on your TV, printed questions for anyone who reads better than they hear.
  • Our sound. A speaker that carries the room without pain. The TV's own speaker never does.
  • Staff at the table. An aide relaying an answer counts.
  • Memory care. Shorter rounds, familiar music, nothing to lose.

Questions people ask

The honest answers.

What does the room need?

A TV we can plug into, an outlet, and the residents where they already sit. We bring the rest.

Is it right for memory care?

Yes, with shorter rounds, music first, questions the room can answer, and staff at the table. Your team decides who joins.

Can we book the same day every month?

Yes. The Monthly Series holds your slot for the year at the same rate.

How do we pay?

An invoice after the visit. Your accounts payable can pay it the way it pays everyone else.
