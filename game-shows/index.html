Questions people ask

The honest answers.

What does the room need?

A TV or projector we can plug into, power, and quiet enough to hear a question.

How many people play at once?

Ten on the Quiz Show board, two teams on the Showdown, four couples, a tile per buzzer in trivia. Tables play as teams.

Can the questions be about us?

Yes: your company, your couple, your school, the guest of honor. Every question is edited before the night, and for schools and senior living, clean is the rule.

Does everyone get a turn?

Contestants change each round, tables play as teams, and the room plays along out loud.

How long does it run?

A show runs sixty to ninety minutes, opener through the last round. Inside that, the segments are short on purpose: nobody is trapped in one game.

Where do you go?

New Bedford is home: the SouthCoast from Fall River and Dartmouth to Wareham, the South Shore, Cape Cod, and Rhode Island to Providence.
