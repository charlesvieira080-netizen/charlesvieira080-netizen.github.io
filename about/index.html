In his own words

The best moments are not on the schedule.

They live in the seconds around it. The look your father gives you before the doors open. The friend who completely loses it during the toast.

Twenty years of running the night teaches you to live one step ahead of it. You stop watching the schedule and start watching people, because you know where the next moment happens. You are the one cueing it.

For years that knowledge had nowhere to go. The night ended, and the couple waited months to see any of it. So filming it became part of the job. The booth stays. The camera is the new tool in the same hands.
