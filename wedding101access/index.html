Wedding 101 · The planning tools

School's in session.

Planning a wedding can be a daunting task. It's like a movie: it's expensive, everyone needs to know their role, and you only get one take. Planning and throwing a fun and memorable wedding with a flowing timeline takes experience. I have twenty years of it, and I want to share it with you.

Wedding 101 is my step-by-step, A to Z guide to planning your wedding, written from the middle of the room. Every article is free on the hub. The four worksheets below go with it, and they do the planning grunt work for you. Enter your name and email and I'll send all four as PDFs.

  • The budget worksheet. Decide what you'd miss most and let the split follow. Percentages first; dollars only if you type a total.
  • The team sheet. Every provider's day-of contact, arrival, departure, and meal on one page, plus who on your side holds what. The contact sheet this page has promised for years.
  • The run of show. Your ceremony time in, the whole night timed out the way I time it, printed for you and for every vendor.
  • The DJ sheet. Every cue and every song from the prelude to the sendoff, with pronunciations, for whoever runs your music.

Not a fan of forms? Every one of these tools is also free on the Wedding 101 hub, no form and no email: fill them in on your phone and print them yourself. Your email goes to me and nowhere else.
