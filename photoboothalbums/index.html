How to get it

One message, and we find it.

  • The date of the event, or as close as you can get.
  • The names on the booking, or the venue.
  • Where to send it. The email on the booking is easiest, but any address works.

We answer from a real inbox, usually within one business day.

Two things worth knowing

Older albums take a little longer.

  • We changed the album system in August 2022. Albums from before then still exist, they are just not in the current archive, so give us a day to pull one.
  • Albums are yours to share. Send the link to anyone who was there. There is nothing to log into and no expiry we impose.

Tell us the date. We'll find the album.

If you have had trouble opening one, say so and we will track it down rather than send the same link back.
