How it plays

Hear it, find it, mark it.

1

Everyone gets a card

Song titles instead of numbers, and no two cards alike. Large print with sixteen squares for activity rooms, twenty-five with a free space for parties.

2

A few seconds of a song. Know it? Mark it. Nobody races anybody, and the good ones get to play a little longer.

3

Somebody yells bingo

We check the card against the calling sheet right there, in front of the room.

Questions people ask

The honest answers.

Do we need a screen?

No. Music bingo runs on sound and cards, so any room with an outlet works.

How many people can play?

Everyone in the room. We print a card for every player, and no two are alike.

Can we pick the songs?

Yes. Send us the list, or give us a decade and we'll build it for your crowd.

Can we play for prizes?

For bragging rights it's easy. If your group wants to play for prizes, Massachusetts licenses bingo through the State Lottery, so check with them before the cards go out.

Create a buzz. Yell bingo.

Tell us the room and the crowd, and we'll build the playlist that fits it.
